## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Source-bound BP04 deadline acceptance environment
The development deadline fixture SHALL bind a fresh isolated project, source/class/JAR inputs, immutable images, configuration, runtime identities and exact owned resources before any probe, SQL control, release or cleanup. It SHALL reject unknown, changed, partial or legacy records without adoption and SHALL preserve private evidence and data on unknown ownership or settlement.

#### Scenario: Reject stale acceptance identity
- **WHEN** a receipt identifies an old image or the current source/mount/container differs from the sealed record
- **THEN** the fixture refuses execution before mutating resources or releasing controls and does not upgrade/adopt the record

### Requirement: Genuine bounded authority gates
The fixture SHALL use seventeen genuine OIDC identities and exact scoped grants/ACLs sealed before application startup.

#### Scenario: Prepare seventeen real identities
- **WHEN** the fixture is sealed before application startup
- **THEN** its17 real OIDC identities have exact persisted and issued issuer/subject, tenant membership and bounded explicit grants without mock authority

### Requirement: Fixed genuine DM relay
A fixed private TLS response relay SHALL forward only the two existing DM sharing POST paths to the one verified upstream with original bearer/tenant binding and genuine responses.

#### Scenario: Reject a caller-selected destination
- **WHEN** a request selects a route or upstream outside the two fixed paths
- **THEN** the relay rejects it before forwarding credentials or body

### Requirement: Bounded relay retention and release
The relay SHALL enforce existing request16384/response32768 byte limits, at most16 held responses and a bounded one-use owned release protocol without arbitrary destination, redirect, forged payload, decompression, unbounded queue or deadline renewal.

#### Scenario: Release a genuine late result
- **WHEN** an actual final DM response is held past its application's deadline and then released
- **THEN** timeout remains a failure stimulus, current authority is preserved, no late success is fabricated and independent settlement/full fact comparison determines the result

### Requirement: Cumulative deadline and admission evidence
The fixture SHALL preserve wholeKB8s, initialDM2s, finalTOTAL2s including finalDM1.5s and local500ms, lock250ms, actor1/global16 single-process/noqueue and admission50ms.

#### Scenario: Preserve selected service limits
- **WHEN** the fixture runs a real deadline case
- **THEN** the product limits remain unchanged and both final subphases inherit the same2s parent

### Requirement: Actual cumulative clock attribution
The fixture SHALL measure actual phase boundaries with one monotonic origin and explicit observation uncertainty, use genuine bounded external transport/row-lock barriers, and report INCOMPLETE when those boundaries cannot be established. Separate reset clocks, component-only timers and browser9s bounds SHALL NOT establish deployed cumulative acceptance.

#### Scenario: Exhaust the common local clock
- **WHEN** successive real local lock waits individually remain below their cap but exhaust the shared local/final deadline
- **THEN** the case records the finite failure and independently settled durable outcome without partial facts, widened timeout or resetting the original clock

### Requirement: Honest pool and SQL ownership observation
A PostgreSQL idle session or absent transaction SHALL NOT be called proof that no Hikari connection is borrowed.

#### Scenario: Inspect an idle PostgreSQL session
- **WHEN** a session is idle with no transaction
- **THEN** the report describes that database observation and does not infer Hikari borrow state

### Requirement: Unselected local management research
Standard exact-pinned Hikari/Java local management SHALL remain research-only until its access profile is separately reviewed and selected; no production test hook, privilege increase, reflection repair or public management listener is permitted.

#### Scenario: Encounter an unavailable management interface
- **WHEN** no reviewed selected observer profile exists or the exact pool bean cannot be read
- **THEN** the gate remains OPEN without privilege increase, reflection, remote listener or configuration repair

### Requirement: Direct pending-wait pool evidence
A transient numeric pool sample SHALL prove the fixed pending-wait point; the required across-wait gate SHALL remain OPEN until direct deployed pending-wait samples, positive borrowed controls and same-factory/pool source binding prove that exact claim. Continuous absence between samples SHALL NOT be claimed.

#### Scenario: Sample a genuine pool while authority is paused
- **WHEN** a reviewed future collector obtains zero active connections at both held HTTP barriers
- **THEN** its receipt is labelled a fixed pending-wait observation and the report states whether a connection was retained across each fixed pending wait using same-pool positive controls and source ownership proof, while continuous absence between samples is not claimed

### Requirement: Independent settlement and original outcome reconciliation
After timeout/abort the fixture SHALL independently settle owned remote/local execution and compare complete scoped domain, command, audit, outbox and counter hashes. Already-dispatched COMMIT without known acknowledgement SHALL be UNKNOWN until durable original-key reconciliation establishes one original outcome; cancellation, rollback attempt or control cleanup SHALL NOT prove absence. Unsettled cases SHALL fail acceptance, retain evidence and refuse further case admission.

#### Scenario: Lose a genuine commit acknowledgement
- **WHEN** a real dispatched COMMIT result is not known to the caller
- **THEN** the fixture neither fabricates rollback nor issues a new key to conceal the outcome and rechecks current authority before reconciling the original key and immutable facts
